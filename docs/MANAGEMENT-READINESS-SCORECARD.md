## Overall

- **Management / presentation readiness: 9.5/10**
- **Website/product readiness before legal sign-off: 9.3/10**
- **Public-production readiness today: 8.7/10**, with the remaining material gate being legal/compliance sign-off plus normal live-server/device QA.

## Commercial perspective

The website is now strong enough to support sales and lead generation because it explains the real Turkelite proposition — full practical journey coordination around clinically confirmed care — rather than looking like a generic clinic directory. Commercial success still depends on qualified traffic, clinic contracts/availability, response speed, real proof/reviews as they become available, pricing, sales follow-up and service delivery.
